First of A Thousand Autumns

This is a fan website to Hypergryth's game Arknights, specifically under the theme of the game patch "First of a Thousand Autumns." This is a non-commmecialized website intending to be a show of love to Arknights as a fan of the game, hoping to help Arknights to gain more audience. Most of the assets used in this website belongs to Arknights or other institutions. However the structure and design of this website are created by me. (Check out my other works if interested!)

Some context for audience who are not familiar with Arknights or its lore in the first place. Arknights can be described as a kind of sci-fi tale set on a planet called Terra. There exists unique species that have different traits and special abilities. Sui is one of them.

Sui itself

Sui is powerful and is classified as a "Feranmut" in the world of Terra, a type of creature that has abilities beyond what one can imagine, and has almost endless lifespan.

To describe Sui's story it would require a lot of details, but to put it simply, in its long living life, Sui has been the dominating one over other Feranmuts, not to mention the human species that are extremely vulnerable to disasters.

However, Sui's ambition to become the strongest Feranmut eventually brings it to betray its own kind when they are in a war with humans and chooses to turn sides. But in the end Sui experiences similar betrayal from the humanity, and despite its undying body, it is broken into twelve pieces, all of whom has their own consciounesses, powers and wills.

The twelve broken pieces of Sui's are called the Sui Proxies. They have magical abilities just like Sui does. Beginning the moment of their birth, the Sui Proxies thinks of only one question: "Who am I?"

The story of Sui Proxies

As the game's protagonist, Doctor, we are the major leader of a pharmaceutical company located inside a moving landship. From this perspective, we don't actually interact with Sui, but a majority of Sui's Proxies are companions of Rhodes Island's after going through various interesting stories.
